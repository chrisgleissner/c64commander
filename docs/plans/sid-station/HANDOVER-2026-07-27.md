## 3. Hard-won facts — read before debugging

**The engine cannot be trusted to report position right after a seek.**
`seekTo` resolves *before* the engine has caught up. Rebase clocks to the
**target**, never to `positionSeconds()` read back.

**A gesture ends on pointerup, pointerleave AND pointercancel.** `endScrub` runs
more than once per release; it needs its re-entry guard.

**React state from an arbitrary page instance is the wrong source of truth.**
Two separate bugs came from this (device-switch stop, and Stop). Anything that
must survive a remount belongs in a module, keyed off where the action really
happens.

**`localSidPlaybackRef.current` is per-page and starts null.** Never
`?.`-chain a stop through it. Use `getLocalSidPlayback()`.

**The subsong is not in the UI.** The app played `Turrican_3.sid` subsong **23**
while `sidplayfp` defaults to 2; 22 of 24 subsongs scored < 0.10. A "wrong tune"
result is usually the wrong subsong — use `match_any_subsong()`.

**CI does not shard with Playwright's `--shard`.** It selects shard *files* via
`scripts/get-playwright-shard-files.mjs`. Shard 2 is exactly
`playwright/playback.spec.ts`. Ask that script before trying to reproduce.

**Regenerating all screenshots produces ~209 changed PNGs**, nearly all
environmental drift. Keep only genuinely affected shots.

**The Play page's `Listen on` control only renders for SID items.** The existing
play screenshots use `scene.mod`, so they never showed it — hence the dedicated
`play/sid-radio/03-listen-on.png` capture that seeds a SID.

## 4. HIL rig

Devices: **Pixel 4** (adb `9B081FFAZ001WX`), **C64U** and **U64** — all usable.
Re-read `/etc/hosts` every session; the IPs are DHCP-volatile and the file has
stale commented entries. A stale `c64u_device_host` in the app pointing at a
**dead IP** cost hours this session: the app timed out, launched nothing, and its
clock ran on regardless. Check the app's host matches the selected saved device.

**The USB microphone is aimed at the Pixel's speaker and works.** Use it.

- `tools/hil/sid_audio_match.py` — matches a capture against a `sidplayfp`
  render (log-mel, 800–3000 Hz, threshold 0.40, auto-scans subsongs). Real
  captures: correct tune **0.725**, wrong tune −0.049, paused −0.042.
  The band matters: at 150–6000 Hz the *correct* tune scored **negative**,
  because a phone speaker gives nothing below ~700 Hz.
- Android media volume **16/25** is the quietest that matches reliably
  (0.542, 6.7 dB over the room floor). These tests share a room — do not run
  louder. Volume 14 is too quiet (3.9 dB over floor).
- **Measure silence honestly.** True floor is ≈ **−42 dBFS**. Early "silence"
  baselines at −32.9 dBFS were not silent and poisoned every comparison.
- `dumpsys audio | grep -c 'state:started'` is the reliable "is the phone
  producing audio" signal. Mic level is not — the mic hears the whole room,
  including the C64U.
- Scratchpad helpers may be gone (`/tmp` is cleaned). `sid_audio_match.py` is in
  the repo; `seek_latency.py`, `sweep.py`, `miccal.py` were scratchpad-only.
  **Write anything worth keeping into `tools/hil/`.**

Other rig notes: hold the host awake with `systemd-inhibit --what=sleep:idle`
(a 4-hour suspend silently truncated a soak and I reported its numbers as real).
Force debug logging **off** for soaks — `enable_flags` now does this, because an
inherited debug flag inflated `skipToLaunchMs` ~6× (3585 ms vs 561 ms) and
produced a phantom audio underrun.

## 5. Known-good verification recipes

- **Overlap**: play locally, tab Home↔Play ×6, assert `dumpsys audio` stays at 1.
- **Device switch**: play on the C64, switch device from Settings, then read the
  old machine's screen over DMA — `GET /v1/machine:readmem?address=0400&length=1000`
  twice, 5 s apart. Byte 80 alternating 32↔160 is only the BASIC cursor; a
  running SID player moves its clock digits around bytes 921–924.
- **Engine switch**: local → C64 should take the phone to **0** streams and the
  mic to ≈ −40 dBFS.
- **Auto-advance**: tap the progress bar at 95%, then watch that it advances with
  ~0 s overrun.
