C64 Commander

Play SID music, run games and demos, mount disks, and control a C64 Ultimate from one app.

What it does

  • Finds your C64 Ultimate on the network and keeps an eye on its health.
  • Reaches every setting the machine has, and the everyday ones without searching.
  • Plays SID music, programs, cartridges and disk images, from your own files, from the machine itself, or from the free archives.
  • Mounts and swaps disks, and rotates through a multi-disk title.
  • Brings the machine's picture and sound into the app, and sends your input back.
  • Explains itself when something will not answer, with health checks, logs and traces.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no servers of its own. The privacy policy lists every network request the app makes.
